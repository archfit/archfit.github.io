Our Vision

Scoring and sustaining goals should feel obvious

ArchFit exists to make scoring and maintaining fitness goals easier, with guidance that adapts to every training level and lifestyle.

Goal Scores

A single score that reflects your goal readiness, updated daily from key signals without noise.

Habit Loops
