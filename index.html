Features

Everything You Need to Optimize Your Training

From daily readiness scores to long-term trend analysis, ArchFit provides the insights you need to reach your peak potential.

Daily Readiness Score

Get an at-a-glance 0-100 readiness score every morning, powered by analysis of your overnight recovery metrics.

  • Real-time calculation
  • Personalized baseline comparison
  • Confidence indicators

Fatigue State Detection

Four-state fatigue classification: Recovered, Normal, Fatigued, and Overreached. Know exactly where you stand.

Recovery Time Prediction

ML-powered estimates of hours until full recovery, helping you plan training intensity with precision.

Training Load Analytics

ACWR (Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio) tracking with historical trends and predictive insights.

Sleep Analysis

Deep sleep metrics, sleep efficiency scores, and recovery correlation analysis.

AI Recommendations

Actionable, personalized suggestions based on your unique physiological patterns and goals.
